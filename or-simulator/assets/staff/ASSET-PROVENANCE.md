# Crew model provenance

The trainee hand meshes, skeletons and skin weights were adapted from the Nabd patient asset. The supervisor uses the same rigged human base with a procedural gown, cap, gloves, and face mask prepared for this simulator. See [Virtual Patient Asset Provenance](../patient/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md) for the source notes on the CC0 patient base. GLB exports were produced with Blender.
