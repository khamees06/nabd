## The screen

Taken from a real session of the ER training room in this application, driven by
`tools/migration/landing-v6/capture-er-session.js` at 1024 × 704 and scale 2 (`__STOP='chat'`, random numbers
seeded with 19: the asthma case «لين الحياري»; demo account «زيد العبداللات»; understanding services off, so the
room answered in its offline mode). The orders were typed into the room's own input; the nurse's reports, the
mother's question, the patient's answer and the monitor are what the room produced. The application's top bar is
cropped off. The case content is fictional training material that a physician has not reviewed yet. No licence
question: it is a screenshot of this project.
